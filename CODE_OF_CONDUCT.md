# Code of Conduct

Be respectful, technically constructive, and considerate of other contributors.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, credential theft, doxxing, or attempts to pressure contributors into unsafe disclosure are not acceptable.

Keep reviews focused on code, evidence, design, and documented behavior. Disagreement is welcome; personal attacks are not.

Security-sensitive reports should use private disclosure channels described in `SECURITY.md`.

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove contributions that violate these expectations and may restrict participation when necessary to protect the project or its contributors.
